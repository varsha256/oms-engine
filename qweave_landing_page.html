How QWeave Works

From idea to execution in seconds. Our engine bridges the gap between natural language and strict state machines.

1. Type Plain English

Describe your business logic naturally. Tell the agent your required states, roles, and allowed transitions.

2. AI Generates JSON

Our LLM agentic layer strictly parses your request, validates constraints, and outputs a structured tenant workflow JSON.

3. Core Quality Review

We don't blindly execute AI output. Every JSON undergoes rigorous analysis and safety checks by QA experts with 20+ years of experience, ensuring absolute reliability and merchant trust.

4. Production Execution

Once verified, the OMS engine handles complete order creation, enforces strict state transitions, and automates invoice generation instantly.
